Basics (the drum kit, the beat, reading notes, how to sit)

1. Meet the drum kit

This is a real drum kit. Your roll-up pad is a small, flat version of it. Tap any part to hear it and to see where it is on your pad.

Tap a drum
Every part of the kit has its own job in the band.

2. Feel the beat

Music has a steady pulse called the beat. Press Play, then try the buttons: they change how many hits fit into each beat.

Four beats make one bar. The loud click is beat 1. BPM means beats per minute: 60 BPM is one beat every second.

3. Read drum music

Drum music is written on five lines called a staff. Each drum has its own place on it. Tap a note to hear which drum it is.

Tap a note above.
Cross or dotA cross is a cymbal. A dot is a drum.
Up or downStems pointing up are played by the hands. Stems pointing down are played by the feet.
Left to rightRead it like a sentence. The numbers under the staff are the count.
The two numbersAt the start: the top number is how many beats are in a bar.

How long is a note?

The beams joining the notes tell you how fast they go. Pick one and press Play: the line shows where you are.

In every lesson the sheet music runs above the falling notes with the same moving line. When a lesson feels easy, tick "Sheet only" to hide the falling notes and play from the sheet.

🎼 Note quiz: which drum is it?

A note appears on the staff. Tap that drum in the pad picture, or hit it on your real pad. Ten notes make one round.

Press Start. Every right answer on the first try is a point.
